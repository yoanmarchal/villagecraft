# Walk mode (first person)

Walking through the village at eye level: toolbar button (walker icon) or `F`; mouse + keyboard only for now (the button is hidden on touch-only screens).

## How it is split

- **Physics is pure** — [src/walk/](../src/walk/), no three, React or store, tested in `walker.test.ts`:
  - [walkConfig.ts](../src/walk/walkConfig.ts): every size and speed, written in metres and converted (1 unit ≈ 2.8 m, a door is 0.71): walker 1.75 m, eyes at 1.62 m, 0.5 m wide, 0.4 m step, walk 1.6 m/s, run 4.5 m/s, 45 cm jump.
  - [walkWorld.ts](../src/walk/walkWorld.ts): the collision world behind the `WalkWorld` interface — `groundAt(x, z)` (terrain + landscape, the same function as the orbit camera floor), `solidsIn(rect)` (axis-aligned boxes), `reach` (radius the walker cannot leave: the flat meadow, `flatRadius`). `createVillageWorld()` makes one box per run of stacked cells of a column, at its building level (`ground.baseAt`); a ground-floor cell reaches far below the terrain (the plinth), an arch does not touch the ground, so lanes under arches stay passable.
  - [walker.ts](../src/walk/walker.ts): `stepWalker(world, state, input, dt)` — the walker is a vertical box, feet at `position`; movement is resolved axis by axis (x, z, then y), so walls stop and the walker slides along them; an obstacle no higher than `STEP_HEIGHT` is climbed, the terrain always carries (never blocks); gravity, jump, ceiling, and the walker sticks to the ground going down slopes and steps. Frames are cut into ≤ 1/120 s substeps (frame-rate independent, tested) and capped at 0.1 s. `findSpawn()` searches the nearest free spot; `isFree()` checks one.
  - [walkInput.ts](../src/walk/walkInput.ts): keys by `KeyboardEvent.code` (physical position), so WASD on QWERTY is ZQSD on AZERTY with no layout table; arrows, Shift (run), Space (jump).
- **Scene** — [src/components/WalkControls.tsx](../src/components/WalkControls.tsx), mounted by `VoxelScene` instead of `CameraFloor` while `uiStore.viewMode === 'walk'`. `OrbitControls` stays mounted but `enabled={false}`: it keeps its target, and on exit the camera position, rotation, `near` and `fov` saved at entry are restored exactly. It spawns at the free spot nearest the orbit target with `SPAWN_CLEARANCE` around it (any free spot if no place is that open — a spot merely free put the walker's nose against a wall), facing the target (the village, from its edge) or, when standing on it, the way the orbit camera looked — turned just enough to have `SPAWN_VIEW` free ahead (`clearHeading`; the "most open" heading turned its back on the village); `near` drops to 0.02 (shell walls are a few cm thick) and `fov` to 70. Look = pointer lock + `movementX/Y` (yaw/pitch, `YXZ` Euler); the canvas uses `frameloop="demand"`, so every frame calls `invalidate()` while the pointer is locked, and nothing renders while paused. If the village changes under the walker (undo, API), it respawns at the nearest free spot.
- **Interaction** — while walking, the click plane has no handlers (no build, no preview), App's letter shortcuts are off (they move the walker), only `F` toggles. `Esc` releases the pointer (the browser always does) = pause, showing [WalkOverlay](../src/components/WalkOverlay.tsx) (controls card, back to orbit); `Esc` again or the button returns to orbit; clicking the view resumes. A crosshair shows while locked. `viewMode` and `walkLocked` live in [uiStore](../src/store/uiStore.ts), never persisted.

## Next steps (not done)

- **Entering houses**: give `createVillageWorld` per-cell walls instead of whole-cell boxes — hollow cells (`isHollowType`) as `WALL_T` wall boxes per `exterior`/`partition` side (`getHollowShape()`), nothing on `open` sides, a gap at `doorFace` (opening size from `OPENING_SIZES`), floor slabs (`FLOOR_T`) as walkable boxes. Stay in `src/walk/`, reading the same `CellContext` data the builders use (don't import builders into the walker).
- **Stairs**: from `stairPlan.ts` (`CellContext.stair`), each step as a box ≤ `STEP_HEIGHT` (16 risers per storey: 1/16 unit ≈ 17 cm, under the 0.4 m step), the stairwell opening cut out of the floor slab.
- **Trees, roofs as shapes, ramparts walks**: more boxes in `solidsIn`; trees as thin trunks (positions from `landscape.ts`).
- **Touch**: virtual stick + drag to look (no pointer lock on mobile).
- **Settings**: mouse sensitivity, invert Y, FOV in the control store (new keys need no migration; changing a default does).
- **API**: expose walk mode on `window.villagecraft` (enter at a point, look) for MCP screenshots at eye level.
