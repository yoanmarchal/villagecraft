# Walk mode (first person)

Walking through the village at eye level, into the houses and up their stairs: toolbar button (walker icon) or `F`; mouse + keyboard only for now (the button is hidden on touch-only screens).

## How it is split

- **Physics is pure** — [src/walk/](../src/walk/), no three, React or store, tested in `walker.test.ts`:
  - [walkConfig.ts](../src/walk/walkConfig.ts): every size and speed, written in metres and converted (1 unit ≈ 2.8 m, a door is 0.71): walker 1.75 m, eyes at 1.62 m, 0.5 m wide, 0.4 m step, walk 1.6 m/s, run 4.5 m/s, 45 cm jump.
  - [walkWorld.ts](../src/walk/walkWorld.ts): the collision world behind the `WalkWorld` interface — `groundAt(x, z)` (terrain + landscape, the same function as the orbit camera floor), `solidsIn(rect)` (axis-aligned boxes), `reach` (radius the walker cannot leave: the flat meadow, `flatRadius`). `createBoxWorld()` indexes the boxes delivered by the build worker in 1-unit buckets.
  - [walker.ts](../src/walk/walker.ts): `stepWalker(world, state, input, dt)`, feet at `position`, resolved axis by axis (x, z, then y): walls stop the walker and it slides along them. **Body, head, feet** — the walker is not one box: a box only blocks if it rises more than `STEP_HEIGHT` above the feet (lower ones are steps); the torso is `WALKER_RADIUS` wide, the top `HEAD_H` only `HEAD_R` (~20 cm head); the walker stands on the highest thing under the **centre** of its feet (`FOOT`), within one step of them, so a toe may overhang a tread. Standing on the highest box under the whole 50 cm footprint climbed two treads early and cracked the head on the stairwell edge; a full-width head could not pass under a stairwell edge or round a spiral. The terrain always carries (never blocks); gravity, jump, ceiling; the walker sticks to the ground going down slopes and steps. Frames are cut into ≤ 1/120 s substeps (frame-rate independent, tested) and capped at 0.1 s. `findSpawn()` searches the nearest free spot; `isFree()` checks one.
  - [walkInput.ts](../src/walk/walkInput.ts): keys by `KeyboardEvent.code` (physical position), so WASD on QWERTY is ZQSD on AZERTY with no layout table; arrows, Shift (run), Space (jump).
- **Collision volumes come from the build** — [cellColliders.ts](../src/walk/cellColliders.ts) is the collision "builder": `cellColliders(ctx)` returns boxes in cell space from the same `CellContext` as the geometry builders, so doors and stairs are exactly where they are drawn. `buildVillage` calls it per cell (in the worker), caches the boxes per building with its parts, and `VillageChunkCache.colliders` (6 floats per world box) travels with every build response (`BuildDelivery.colliders`, transferred) → `VillageMeshes` `onColliders` → `VoxelScene` builds the walk world from the colliders of the village **shown**. Per cell:
  - roofs and arches are solid; the bottom cell of a column reaches 50 below its level (the plinth); an arch does not touch the ground, so lanes under arches stay passable;
  - hollow cells: floor slab (top = `STAIR_FLOOR_Y`, the floorboards) and ceiling slab, both pierced by stair openings (not ladder hatches), rasterised in 1/32 strips where a strip is solid only if wholly outside the opening — the edge falls on the open side (a centre test left 3 cm of slab that stopped the head on the L stair, and the 1.5 cm ring of floor between a spiral's opening and the tower wall caught the shoulders); one `WALL_T` wall per `exterior`/`partition` side, pillars, rounded corners filled from the inner arc outwards in slices both ways; the door passage (opening width × `openingScale`, floorboards to lintel, `DOOR_CUT_DEPTH` deep so it also cuts a tower's curved corners) is subtracted from those walls (`subtractBox`); door steps on slopes (`doorSteps`, shared with the builder);
  - stairs: L = solid lower flight, winders rasterised by sector, upper flight solid underneath; spiral = newel + steps rasterised on a 14 × 14 grid, each as thick as drawn (r + 3 cm) — solid down to the floor, the storey above's spiral blocked the head of the one below; railings on the `free` edges of the arrival opening, cut back `RAIL_TRIM` from the arrival edge (the landing strip between opening and wall is ~30 cm: with the full railing the walker was stuck at the top). Ladders, furniture, fireplaces and door panels do not collide (yet).
  - Tested in `cellColliders.test.ts` on real builds: entering by the door onto the floorboards, a wall stops, climbing an L stair and a spiral (on the outer side of the treads) to the floor above.
- **Doors open on approach** — the moving parts of a door (panel, planks, strap hinges, ring: `Part.door`, a `DoorHinge`) are not merged into the chunks: one group per door and material (`door:<cell>|<mat>`, 2 per door — wood, iron; `MergedGroup.door` carries the hinge in world space). Hinge on the inner edge of the panel, on the `doorHingeSide()` side: left by default, right if the open panel would sweep the cell's stair or stairwell ([doors.ts](../src/render/cells/doors.ts), which also holds `openingScale`, `doorSteps`, `doorSwingArea`, `doorPose`). `WalkControls` publishes the feet in [walkerPresence](../src/walk/walkerPresence.ts) (module object, no React render); `VillageMeshes` swings each door to `DOOR_OPEN_ANGLE` (100°, inwards) when the walker is within `DOOR_OPEN_DISTANCE` of its sill, closes it otherwise (and when leaving walk mode), `invalidate()`-ing while any door moves. In orbit view doors stay closed: the outside look is unchanged. Tested: an open panel stays inside its cell, clear of the walls.
- **Scene** — [src/components/WalkControls.tsx](../src/components/WalkControls.tsx), mounted by `VoxelScene` instead of `CameraFloor` while `uiStore.viewMode === 'walk'`. `OrbitControls` stays mounted but `enabled={false}`: it keeps its target, and on exit the camera position, rotation, `near` and `fov` saved at entry are restored exactly. It spawns at the free spot nearest the orbit target with `SPAWN_CLEARANCE` around it (any free spot if no place is that open — a spot merely free put the walker's nose against a wall) — often inside a hall now that interiors are walkable — facing the target or, when standing on it, the way the orbit camera looked, turned just enough to have `SPAWN_VIEW` free ahead (`clearHeading`; the "most open" heading turned its back on the village); `near` drops to 0.02 (shell walls are a few cm thick) and `fov` to 70. Look = pointer lock + `movementX/Y` (yaw/pitch, `YXZ` Euler); the canvas uses `frameloop="demand"`, so every frame calls `invalidate()` while the pointer is locked, and nothing renders while paused. If the village changes under the walker (undo, API), it respawns at the nearest free spot.
- **Interaction** — while walking, the click plane has no handlers (no build, no preview), App's letter shortcuts are off (they move the walker), only `F` toggles. `Esc` releases the pointer (the browser always does) = pause, showing [WalkOverlay](../src/components/WalkOverlay.tsx) (controls card, back to orbit); `Esc` again or the button returns to orbit; clicking the view resumes. A crosshair shows while locked. `viewMode` and `walkLocked` live in [uiStore](../src/store/uiStore.ts), never persisted.

## Next steps (not done)

- **Ladders and attics**: climbing (a ladder as a vertical "step" zone), the gable attic as a walkable volume under the roof slopes.
- **Furniture and fireplaces as obstacles**: boxes from the furniture footprints (`furnitureParts` already computes them for its own placement).
- **Smoother stairs**: the camera jumps one riser (~17 cm) per tread; ease the eye height.
- **Trees, roofs as shapes, rampart wall-walks**: more boxes; trees as thin trunks (positions from `landscape.ts`).
- **Touch**: virtual stick + drag to look (no pointer lock on mobile).
- **Settings**: mouse sensitivity, invert Y, FOV in the control store (new keys need no migration; changing a default does).
- **API**: expose walk mode on `window.villagecraft` (enter at a point, look) for MCP screenshots at eye level.
